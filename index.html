Frequently Asked Questions.

Everything you need to know before discovering your signature scent.

Consider the notes you naturally enjoy. Fresh citrus and floral notes offer a lighter experience, while amber, vanilla and woods create a warmer character.
Longevity depends on the fragrance composition, concentration, skin chemistry and application. Some fragrances remain noticeable for several hours.
Apply fragrance to pulse points such as your wrists and neck. Avoid rubbing your wrists together after application.
Contact us by email or phone to enquire about our collection and available ordering options.
Keep your fragrance in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and sudden temperature changes.
